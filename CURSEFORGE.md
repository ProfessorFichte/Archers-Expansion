## 🕹️ Features

These archery sub-classes enhance the experience for every ranged weapon enjoyer!

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfessorFichte/Archers-Expansion/1.21.1/.media/images/archerse_showcase.png" alt="Archers Expansion Showcase">
</div>

### Spells

Make use of the wide range of new archery skills to make fighting and combat more fun: equip a Spell Book and unlock all of its powerful spells!

To get one, find a Spell Binding Table in village <a href="https://modrinth.com/mod/gazebos">Gazebos</a>, or build your own (just don't forget the bookshelves around the Spell Binding Table).

To use a spell book, equip it and hold a weapon (vanilla or from third-party mods). All spells are listed in the <a href="https://github.com/ProfessorFichte/Archers-Expansion/wiki">Wiki</a>.

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfessorFichte/Archers-Expansion/1.21.1/.media/images/archerse_spellbinding.png" alt="Archers Expansion Spell Binding">
</div>

**Deadeye's List**

The skills of a bounty hunter: fast and tricky shots, decoys and harmful arrows that inflict bad status effects.

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfessorFichte/Archers-Expansion/1.21.1/.media/images/deadeye_spells.png" alt="Deadeye Spells">
</div>

**Tundric Hunting**

Freezing arrows with large area effects that deal frost damage and keep the enemy slow, and a Polar Bear that fights by your side.

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfessorFichte/Archers-Expansion/1.21.1/.media/images/tundra_hunter_spells.png" alt="Tundra Hunter Spells">
</div>

**War Archery Instructions**

High burst damage, explosive arrows and bulky armor for a safer archery playstyle.

<div align="center" style="text-align:center">
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfessorFichte/Archers-Expansion/1.21.1/.media/images/war_archer_spells.png" alt="War Archer Spells">
</div>

New armor sets with archery bonuses for the new sub-classes, from the basic sets up to netherite.
